Every screen the plan promises, and whether it is drawn

The plan and the conversations so far add up to 47 functions. Two are built and work as they are, eight are built and need a redesign, 34 are drawn on this page, and three wait on a decision from you. This page checks each function against a screen, reviews what is live today on a phone, and draws the rest in the product's own look. Sample data is marked as such. Nothing drawn here is built.

2 · Review of what is live

Fourteen findings on the screens that exist, and what already works

Measured on a 390 px phone against the running Arena, not guessed. Severity reflects what stops a new member from reaching or understanding something.

HIGH

The Arena sits inside the owner's navigation

nav at 390 px: Live, Bunnies, Lab & warren memory, Arena (x 430–476), Admin. Screen is 390 wide.

Members see operator links, and the Arena tab is off screen with no hint that the bar scrolls. Fix: a separate Arena shell with its own brand and a bottom tab bar (S1).

HIGH

Locked options lead nowhere

Zombies, Gods, Momentum, extra bots: all say "Pro", none is tappable.

A member who wants more hits a dead end, and there is no plan page at all. Fix: a paywall sheet that explains the pack or limit and links to plans (S10).

HIGH

New members land on an empty card

First screen after the link: "You have no bunny yet". The free AI design is one tap deeper, inside the form.

The best feature is hidden at the moment it matters. Fix: landing, consent, then a three-step first run that starts with the AI (S2, S3).

HIGH

No consent or legal entry points

Sign-in has a "paper trading only" line. No terms, privacy notice or age and risk acknowledgement.

Needed before public launch, and before the leaderboard shows other people's names. Fix: consent asked once after the first link, so the form still cannot reveal who has an account (S2). Text needs a lawyer.

MED

Creating a bot is one very long page

form height 1,927 px at 390 px wide: nine groups, about 2.4 screens.

People lose their place and cannot see what they are building. Fix: a five-step flow with a live preview card, and options a plan cannot use greyed with the reason, not an error afterwards (S5).

MED

A bot card is a dead end

No detail view. Version history exists in the API with no screen. Rules clip at three lines. No pause.

Fix: a bot page with performance, decisions, versions and settings (S6), and Pause and Stop in reach (S4, S17).

MED

One product, four names, wrong nouns

"Create a bunny" appears for Cats, Robots and Zombies. The same thing is called bunny, bot, Warren, race track, Arena and beebots.

Fix: "bot" in the product's chrome, the theme's own noun on the card ("Cat · Trend"), one name for the place. Needs your call (see Decisions).

MED

Leaderboard rows go nowhere

Rows are not tappable. League chips show a bare count with no label. No search. The member's own row can be far down the page.

Fix: tappable rows to public profiles, a pinned "You" row, labelled leagues, handle search, season archive (S7, S8).

MED

Touch targets below a comfortable size

primary button 41 px, chips 37, tabs 37, Edit and Delete 30 px tall and side by side. (WCAG 2.2 minimum is 24 px, so these pass it; 44 px is the usual phone guideline.)

Fix: 44 px minimum everywhere, destructive actions moved away from edit (S6, S15).

MED

The product speaks English only

The owner and likely first members write Portuguese. No language setting exists.

A decision before copy is final, since every string in these mockups would be translated. Fix: language setting in S15; pt-BR first is the likely call.

MED

"Why did it do that?" has no honest answer yet

The decision model returns a choice and odds. It returns no sentence.

Showing invented reasoning would be wrong. Fix: show what really exists: the inputs, the odds for each move, and any risk rule that changed the outcome (S6).

LOW

Loading, empty and error states are plain text

"Loading…" and "The Arena is not reachable right now." in a card, often with no retry.

Fix: skeletons, designed empties and errors with a retry and a cause (S17).

LOW

Avatar glyphs differ by device

Avatars are system emoji on a coloured ring.

Fine as a placeholder. The painted packs replace them; the layouts here already leave the space.

LOW

Status copy is dense

"LONG BTC $622.00, -$0.03 open. 3 decisions. Last call: Hold the winner (82% sure)."

Fix: split into a state pill, three figures and one secondary line (S4, S6).

PASSES

What already works

contrast: body 10.4:1, muted text 5.3:1, status colours 4.7 to 9.0:1, all above 4.5:1. No horizontal scroll at 390 px.

Keep: locked options shown rather than hidden, status always paired with a glyph or word, private-by-default wording, the anti-enumeration sign-in.

Decisions

  • Status is the whole list: Draft, Verifying, Live, Needs changes. A member never has to guess what happens next.
  • The checklist mirrors the plan's verification: a real-data test, then a paper period. Steps done are checked; the one that blocks is named with days left.
  • Free skills can be published by anyone. Paid options are visible and locked for Free members, with the same sheet as everywhere (S10).
  • Seller money uses a payment provider's hosted onboarding for identity and tax, so those screens are the provider's, not ours.
  • The studio editor itself is today's Lab workspace. It needs a member-facing skin and a sandbox, which is a build item, not a design gap.

Decisions

4 · Decisions for you

What blocks the final design

Each one changes words or screens on this page. My recommendation comes first.

1

Language

Portuguese (Brasil) first, English second. Every string here is English. Translating after the layouts are final costs less than translating twice, but only if we settle the nouns first (next item).

2

The nouns

"Bot" in the chrome, the theme's noun on the card. One name for the place: "Arena". The owner's Warren stays the owner's. "Race track" becomes plain words such as "running".

3

Pause versus Stop

Pause keeps positions under their stops; Stop closes them. Both exist on paper. Matters more if live trading ever arrives.

4

Downgrade

Extra bots are paused, never deleted, and kept 30 days. The alternative (delete) is cheaper to build and will make people angry.

5

Reviews and ratings

Skip at launch. Drawn on the skill page, but the plan has verification and reports, not stars. Reviews invite fake ones and need moderation.

6

Follow and comments

Not in v1. You called this a social platform. The plan's social pieces are profiles, leagues, badges and shared rules. Follows and comments add moderation work and are the usual first abuse channel.

7

Templates

Three, written by us. They are content, need your review, and should be labelled as examples, not advice.

8

Legal text and contact

A lawyer writes the terms, privacy notice, age line, affiliate rules and risk notice. The screens have the places; they do not have the words.

9

Still open from the plan

Price and payment processor, countries, who pays model costs on Pro, hosted only or also a self-run option. Screens S10, S11 and S13 wait on these.
